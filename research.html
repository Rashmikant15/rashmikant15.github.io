Research

Research areas

My work combines physics-based simulation, artificial intelligence, optimization, and high-performance computing to solve engineering problems across scales.

Scientific Machine Learning / Physics-AI

Developing surrogate and geometry/physics-aware models for engineering prediction, simulation acceleration, and data-driven analysis. Current interests include deep learning for CFD/CAE, neural operators, interpretable model discovery, and digital twins.

SciMLSurrogatesPINNsDigital Twins

Energy-aware AI/HPC Systems

Designing multi-objective scheduling algorithms for GPU/VM placement in AI/HPC data centers, balancing utilization, response time, service-level constraints, and renewable/grid energy signals.

GAVM SchedulingGPUaaSGreen Computing

Computational Mechanics & Multiphysics

Numerical modeling of nonlinear structural dynamics, CFD, fluid-structure interaction, and coupled fluid-structure-piezoelectric-circuit systems, with emphasis on robust computational workflows.

FEMFVMFSIMultiphysics

Multidisciplinary Aero-Structural Optimization

Custom optimization frameworks integrating aerodynamic loads, structural response, inertia relief, buckling/post-buckling assessment, and composite design for lightweight aircraft structures.

MDOCFRP/CFRTPBuckling

Bio-inspired & Plant-inspired Aerial Robotics

Design, modeling, microfabrication, and performance evaluation of flapping-wing nano air vehicles and plant-inspired micro air vehicles, including hierarchical coupled simulation.

BiomimeticsMicro Air VehiclesMicromachining

GPU-accelerated Scientific Computing

Improving CAE/CFD and post-processing performance through GPU computing, workflow automation, resource management, and scalable scientific-data pipelines.

CUDA/GPUHPCAutomation

Research vision

My longer-term goal is to unify high-fidelity modeling, AI-assisted optimization, efficient GPU/HPC execution, and experimental validation. This creates a path toward faster engineering design cycles, resource-efficient scientific computing, stable untethered bio-inspired robots, and sustainable adaptive aerospace structures.
